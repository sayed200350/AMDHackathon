# Counsel-in-a-Box

Drop a folder of contracts, get a senior-associate-grade legal review memo in 90 seconds.

Powered by Qwen3-32B on AMD Instinct MI300X via vLLM.

## How It Works

1. Upload your deal package (contracts, side letters, amendments)
2. Five specialist AI agents analyze the full corpus in parallel
3. Get a structured legal memo with risks, obligations, cross-document issues, and recommendations

## Agents

- **Clause Extractor** — identifies all clauses by type with citations
- **Risk Scorer** — flags deviations from market-standard language
- **Obligation Tracker** — extracts every deadline and deliverable
- **Cross-Reference Auditor** — finds contradictions across documents
- **Memo Drafter** — synthesizes everything into a partner-ready memo
